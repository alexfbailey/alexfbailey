She thrifts an entire wardrobe on a $200 budget — and shoots it like a film.

Alex Bailey is a filmmaker, photographer and content creator whose thrift-with-me videos, thrift flips and designer-spotting series have built an audience of more than 145,000 across TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and Facebook.

What separates the work from the category is production. Alex directs commercials for a living — for Aeroplan, Tim Hortons, RBC, McDonald's, Cetaphil and Pretty Litter — and brings the same crew standard to a sixty-second vertical. She shoots, directs and edits everything herself, which means brand content arrives broadcast-ready rather than as a first draft.

She also produced êmîcêtôsêt: Many Bloodlines, a TIFF Canada's Top Ten selection and winner of the Hot Docs Betty Youson Award, and directed the Citytv one-hour documentary You've Been Posted. She is the author of Thrift Book, a sustainable-style magazine and journal.

Sustainability isn't a campaign angle here — it is the entire premise of the channel, and the audience arrived for it.

iv · What gets made
01

The $200 Budget Challenge

A full seasonal wardrobe built on a fixed budget, shot as one narrative. The series the audience returns for, and the easiest place for a brand to sit inside a story rather than beside one.

02

Under $20 occasionwear

The highest-performing format on the channel: one dress, one budget, one deadline. Two have passed a million views.

03

Real or Thrift?

Designer authentication on the rack — spotting the real thing among the racks, and explaining how. High saves, high comments, strong completion.

04

Thrift flips

A piece bought, altered and re-styled end to end. Naturally hosts tools, materials, care and laundry products without a hard read.

05

Long-form thrift-with-me

Eight to fifteen minutes on YouTube, where an integration can breathe for sixty to ninety seconds and still feel like part of the trip.

06

Set diaries

Behind the scenes from commercial and documentary shoots — the proof, in public, that the production quality is real.
